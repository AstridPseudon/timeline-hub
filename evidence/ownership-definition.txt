Field definition:
1. Select one occurrence O from the authenticated sequential parent input. Its
   physical edge is occurrence:O/shot -> (shot_id S, shot_revision_id R).
   shot-revision:R must have shot_id S, revision_id R and a verified payload
   digest. Its internal_timeline_revision_id pins the exact child image. An
   image may have multiple owners; selecting O does not transfer sibling edges.
2. The native authoring placement omits shot_revision_id/revision_id. Its local
   shot_id A is S when only one input occurrence has shot_id S; otherwise it is
   identity('shot-placement', project_id, timeline_id, O, S). This is a local
   editing identity, not a replacement physical-source assertion. The native
   occurrence:O/shot shot_id read remains S. The complete projected row must
   equal this derivation, including every other authored/opaque field.
3. Fork reserves destination D, creates shot:D with kind=shot and owner=None,
   and writes occurrence:O.shot_id from A to D. It does not create O anew. The
   source internal payload and the source shot payload copied/remapped for D
   form the allocation baseline. Source S/R, destination D and occurrence O
   have distinct roles; no sibling or final accepted payload can replace them.
4. The next command's intermediate image is derived from that birth, before its
   add-media writes. Internal revision identity includes (project, timeline, D,
   initial_internal); shot revision identity includes (project, D, initial_shot
   with that internal pin). Parent identity includes exact sequential images
   and retained manifest inputs. Foreign/future aliases cannot allocate roots.
5. Production identity_scope is passed from Runtime's authenticated source by
   validate(), never from request annotations. A projected local ID requires a
   nonempty two-string scope. Valid-but-foreign shared scope is also rejected.
   The pre-existing source-only low-level command_output test has no declared
   projection and uses its already-bound physical owner; it cannot authorize a
   local alias. This is not an ABI downgrade or a submit compatibility policy.
