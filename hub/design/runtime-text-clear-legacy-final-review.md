`text/plain; charset=utf-8`. Even an authentic retained legacy object fails
that path. Retained `text/plain` reuse is restricted internally to the computed
project/shot/kind/slot binding, a verified complete hash chain and anchor, and a
matching PRESENT current or historical event. Absent events cannot authorize
reuse. Original main events may omit scope/size/MIME; the binding identity and

binding IDs, event IDs, inline text or trust flags. Both the current-before
