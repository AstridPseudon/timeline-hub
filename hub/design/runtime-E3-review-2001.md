## Precise findings

1. **Required code reduction is absent.** Audit lines 49–61 retain every safeguard and offer caller-side hints or deletion of already-retired paths without naming a live deletion, replacement size or net LOC. This does not meet E-PLAN's 20:02 amendment. Keeping the guards is reasonable; keeping duplicate implementations is a separate decision. The finite replacement proposal below addresses that distinction without inventing a removable safeguard.

2. **Native coverage is overstated.** Audit line 42 says a provider omission requires comparison against generated records and persisted ABI; line 51 refers generally to keeping full capture coverage. That is a requirement to preserve existing checks, not evidence Runtime proves complete native coverage at submission. D62 explicitly declines that new trust surface. Runtime `timeline_commit_store.py:1229–1236` derives and freezes ABI from the accepted physical bindings, with a comment explicitly excluding a separate native-completeness obligation. `tests/test_timeline_recorder_abi_authority.py:145–205`, `test_native_coverage_omissions_at_submit_keep_abi_two`, deliberately accepts the omission packets and requires ABI 2 plus exact replay. The correction is: preserve all current completeness/order checks and persisted ABI, preserve strict fork/inverse use-time refusal, and retain the documented submit-time gap. Do not convert that positive into a refusal test or propose a native attestation seam. A hostile authenticated producer is not fully solved by one writer.

3. **The claimed mounted App chain is inaccurate.** Audit line 31 routes `useTimelineSave` through `createTimelineCommandSession` and `operationHost.transmit`. Actual mounted local saving is `useTimelineSave.ts:319–323` → `createLocalSaveSession` with `send: provider.commitTimeline` → `dataProvider.ts:751–767` → `client.ts:735–741` → generated `commitTimeline` at `generated.ts:202–204`. `commitSession.ts:238–250` selects `operation.transmit` only for placement continuations; its ordinary branch uses `options.send`. The production-source search found no call to `createTimelineCommandSession` outside its definition; its direct callers in this tree are tests. It therefore cannot substantiate an all-path production recommendation. No App preflight/status handling is approved for removal by this review.

4. **Two distinct non-editing custody subjects need separate labels.** D64/D66 concern test-run caller provenance (`CUSTODY_PREFIX`, recipe/env guards and the plain stack manifest). Audit lines 33/47 instead describe Runtime's shipped local-process launch/cleanup custody: `local_worker_composition.py:467–515` creates the role broker and strips/injects `ASTRID_RUNTIME_CUSTODY_*`; `daemon.py:309–323` constructs the production launcher with its preparer/inspector. These controls are orthogonal to timeline authorization. D66 does not authorize deleting them, and passing their tests proves neither commit authorization nor receipt authenticity. Conversely, D66 does not require retaining superseded test-launch custody machinery. This E3 addendum changes neither subject and claims no harness LOC saving.

These findings do not dispute the settled one-writer decision. They limit what follows from it: one acceptance writer removes competing publication authorities, not hostile input, corrupt accepted history, ambiguous aliases or independent process-lifecycle threats.

## Concrete reduced-code recommendation (after ZIP only)

**A. Share the parent projection comparison.** The blocks in Runtime `timeline_commit_auth.py:2216–2224` and `2231–2239` are 9 nonblank physical lines each, identical except indentation and refusal message. Extract one private function taking the current `result`, `following`, `identity_scope`, `manifest` and the original message. It computes the expected parent identity, selects every parent input pin, requires nonempty pins and unanimous exact revision/digest equality, then assigns the expected revision exactly as today. Keep two calls at the existing locations. In particular, do not hoist the validation out of the promotion loop or combine it with the final created-root check: those can observe different intermediate states. Retain both refusal messages byte-for-byte.

The helper needs approximately 9 nonblank lines and the two calls approximately 4, replacing 18: **about 5 nonblank code lines removed**, approximately 3 physical lines after helper spacing. This is body sharing, not removal of either guard. Existing tests above cover real created-child/parent promotion and hostile owner/alias cases; `test_timeline_commit_sequential_verifier.py::test_coordinated_capture_record_map_and_digest_forgery` checks coordinated forged projections. Specific coverage of both original parent-check locations and their distinct messages remains to be confirmed in implementation review.

**B. Share the complete physical-owner enumeration.** The loops at auth `521–526` and `2451–2458` total 14 nonblank physical lines. Extract only the common enumeration into a private helper receiving the boundary, physical ref and the already-selected occurrence rows. Preserve the caller's exact row retrieval: strict `bound["parent"]["payload"]["occurrences"]` in `validate_bindings`, and `.get("occurrences", [])` in `_capture_owners`. This avoids adding a default or changing malformed-input handling. The helper returns the complete list in occurrence order, retaining duplicates; it must not use a set or the first-owner `_physical_owner` function. Preserve the existing `shot is not None` condition and direct internal revision lookup.

Keep `_capture_document_images` and the nonempty-owner refusal in `_capture_owners`. Keep the existing set comparison/message in `validate_bindings`; do not strengthen it into an ordered comparison as part of cleanup. Do not fold the inverse owner comprehensions at 1108–1112 or 1720–1724 into this helper: their strict lookup/coercion behavior differs and is outside the finite proposal. Approximately 8 helper lines plus 2 call lines replace 14: **about 4 nonblank code lines removed**, approximately 2 physical lines after spacing. The retained-real-capture owner mutation and complete-correspondence tests above, plus the original audit's grouped inverse and ambiguity controls, are proving targets; empty/multiple-owner and malformed-row behavior must also be checked before implementation approval.

**Honest total:** target **9 fewer nonblank production-code lines, roughly 1–5 fewer physical repository lines** after spacing/comments. Test/schema/generated/pin/contract changes are zero in this proposal; it claims no E1 cross-repo savings and no historical deletions. This estimate is intentionally small, not a claim of large architectural simplification. If proving the exact old behavior requires new test lines, count them too: the eventual patch qualifies as E3 reduction only if its actual total added lines are fewer than deleted lines. Otherwise report that slice as failing the reduction requirement; do not weaken tests or compress code artificially to meet a count. Given the small saving, folding this into the later E1 cleanup is more proportionate than a standalone security refactor.

## Contract and authority invariants for both slices

The complete C0 wire remains executable as specified, without a replacement DTO:

- Request retains `api_version`, project/timeline scope, exact base revision/publication pair, ordered commands (`schema_version`, `op`, `version`, `select`, `args` and applicable claims), label, steps (`id`, `label`, `intent`, `tags`, `command_ordinals`, `patch`, optional reads), merge, optional batch reads/dry-run, source/rationale/group annotations and HTTP `Idempotency-Key`.
- Success remains `{data, receipt}`: accepted/merged status, exact base/previous/accepted identities, `rebased_onto`, full canonical closure/digest, normalized commands, `id_map`, provider/compiler versions, complete steps/dependencies, effective merge mode, authenticated publisher and annotations. Publication identity comes from `receipt.receipt_id`; preserve receipt key/hash, command kind, project/sequence/event IDs/creation metadata, and `receipt.result == data`. Historical `rebased` receipts replay unchanged under C0 compatibility.
- Conflict/invalid/refused preserve HTTP status, outer code/message and details including status/reason/code/path/hint/suggested command/base/current/conflicts. Each existing refusal remains at the same validation boundary with its original message.
- Preview retains the complete candidate/digest, evaluated head, would-status, normalized commands/map/steps/merge mode and null receipt; no durable side effects.
- No defaults change: ordinary merge remains fields, dry-run false, existing omission normalization stays, and omitted reads on free patches still trigger conservative atomic scopes. D12 validity scope, C0 absent/null/list rules, opaque meta preservation, guarded undo and attribution remain unchanged. D47 excludes mutable validation/history memos; D62 keeps strict authenticated ABI classification, genuine ABI1 replay and ABI2 use-time refusal, including the documented submit-time completeness gap.
