**The hybrid is the right architecture, but this is not yet a dependable execution plan.** It reuses the commit engine, removes the TypeScript executor, and gives agents a general save path. However, several essential behaviors have no implementation owner, the dependency graph omits shared files and consumer migrations, and the operating rules contradict “all merges and reviews on Codex.” **16–24 hours is plausible as a conditional target, not a reliable commitment.** The most likely stall is A2: preserving dependent edits, original guards and restart recovery after removing the executor. The explicitly deferred rendering work also means this ships a narrower foundation, not completed original M4.

**Top five changes, in order:**

1. **Make H3 an end-to-end feasibility check.** Prove the mixed-command queue and real-document patch path together. This exposes the likeliest stall before deletions spread.
2. **Finish the executable contract and name its owners.** Include read guards, merge modes, closure coverage, partial undo and bounded transform reruns. Runtime dry-run already exists ([commit.py:1061](/home/user/work/agent/astrid/sdk/timeline/commit.py:1061)); extend it.
3. **Repair the task graph.** Assign provider files, generated-client adoption, every writer migration and final delivery. Serialize shared-file merges while running genuinely independent work concurrently.
4. **Protect acceptance; cut incidental cleanup.** Do not cut migrations or tests of promised behavior. Keep the signed parity deferral visible; one Composition artifact does not establish one evaluator.
5. **Enforce dispatch-only Claude roles.** Route code, reviews and merges through Codex; derive status from the board and worker results.

The vision is substantially served for editing. It remains incomplete for automatic relational timing, immediate visibility of Agent edits, and unified rendering. Open `meta` stores extensible data; it does not supply reference integrity or the original extension model. Those are honest deferrals, provided the delivery claims say so.
