+## Blocking findings
+
+### 1. Post-start ownership is not established before cleanup can run
+
+`real_pack_host.py:95-106` calls production `ensure_pack_host`, then performs a
+child check and reads the current `generic-host.json` before constructing the
+`RealPackHost` handle.  The `try` whose `BaseException` path calls
+`handle.close()` starts only at line 107.  A concurrent launcher can replace
+the registration after `ensure_pack_host` releases its lock and before line
+105.  If the replacement marker is read, line 109 raises, but line 121 then
+calls `close()` on a handle containing the replacement state.  `close()` sees
+that replacement as the current registration and can retire/signal the other
+launcher’s process group.  This violates the helper’s stated “only its own
+process group” ownership boundary.
+
+Bounded fix: reacquire the existing pack-host lock while adopting the marker,
+and do not construct or close a cleanup handle unless the marker is proven to
+name the exact `result["host_pid"]`/birth identity returned by the production
+launch.  On any mismatch, refuse without calling `close()` on the newly read
+state; let the production registration/retirement path reconcile the raced
+host.  Keep the equality check in `close()` as the final guard.
+
+### 2. A signal can strand the fresh host before the cleanup handle exists
+
+`real_pack_host.py:95-106` runs after `ensure_pack_host` has returned a ready
+host, but before the handle-owning `try` at line 107 and before the outer CLI
+`with` at line 139.  The CLI installs handlers at lines 132-138 that raise
+`SystemExit`.  SIGTERM, SIGINT, or the 180-second SIGALRM during `waitpid`,
+marker adoption, or the intervening instructions exits without invoking
+`RealPackHost.close()`, leaving the host registration/process behind.  The
+production `ensure_pack_host` protects its own startup wait, but it cannot
+protect this post-return window.
+
+Bounded fix: put the entire post-`ensure_pack_host` adoption sequence under a
+cleanup guard that already knows the exact child identity, or defer the three
+termination signals while that guard is installed and replay the pending
+signal after ownership has been established/cleaned.  The guard must refuse a
+registration mismatch without adopting or stopping the replacement.
+
+### 3. Signals are still live during owned cleanup
+
+`real_pack_host.py:132-138` leaves the raising signal handler installed while
+`RealPackHost.__exit__` calls `close()` (`lines 69-73`), and the same is true
+for the exception cleanup at lines 120-121.  A signal during
+`_retire_host_locked`, group termination, or `waitpid` interrupts cleanup with
+`SystemExit`; `close()` has no deferred-signal/cleanup shield.  The helper can
+therefore report neither a clean stop nor a durable cleanup failure while its
+own group or markers remain.
+
+Bounded fix: make `close()` signal-safe on Linux by temporarily blocking or
+deferring SIGTERM/SIGINT/SIGALRM around the existing production retirement,
+group-survivor check, and wait, then restore the handler and deliver the
+pending termination after cleanup.  Preserve the existing identity checks and
+surface cleanup failure rather than swallowing it.
