source byte delta (0 bytes applied). proposed-v3.patch is a review candidate,
not an application recommendation: its fast intermediate-reload control fails
the journey's original posts=1 guard. The retained test asserts that failure;

observed counts is outside the brief. Raising counts is not proposed.

Why the remaining lifecycle is unsafe
-------------------------------------
useTimelineSave.ts:362-366 automatically recovers a sole durable local-save
owner. localSaveSession.ts:322-325 claims that owner and pumps its persisted
request. commitOutbox.ts:240-251 lists persisted owners without filtering them
by an editor fixture's recovery button. This applies to the ORIGINAL
page.reload() as well as the later reopened page.

D53 has already observed [A,A] / forwarded[A] before B is authored. If reload
recovery runs before the subsequent storage reads/close, the original fault
helper classifies its exact A as recovery and forwards it to the OLD Runtime.
The candidate therefore fails posts=1 before restart. If that reload is armed
and held instead, closing it and mounting again after restart produces FOUR
observed attempts before release; this fails D75's exact THREE. Both schedules
are retained. The passing P1 is a useful automatic-recovery comparator, but its
single recovery mount and lack of a dependent B do not prove this P3 lifecycle.
No inference is made that this fast schedule occurred in actual26907.
